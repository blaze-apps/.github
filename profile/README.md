# Products by BlazeApps

We build software for app developers, restaurant operators, and music makers.

### [AppDrift](https://appdrift.co)

App store optimization for indie developers and small studios. Track keyword rankings, create store screenshots, draft and translate listing metadata, and manage App Store and Google Play publishing.

[Website](https://appdrift.co) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@appdriftco) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/app-drift/)

### [Marji](https://marji.co)

Restaurant food cost software that connects supplier prices to recipe costs. Review ingredient quantities, yields and costs, follow supplier price changes, and decide which dishes need attention. Includes a free hosted QR menu.

[Website](https://marji.co) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@getmarji) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/marji-co/)

### [monody](https://monody.ai)

A DAW with an AI accompanist built in. Ask for changes in your session and review the notes, devices and automation the assistant edits. Early access.

You lead. monody accompanies.
